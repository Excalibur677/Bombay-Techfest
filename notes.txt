------------------------------------------------
VOICE GUIDE
------------------------------------------------
Tone:       Cold. Authoritative. Sparse.
Voice:      A system talking to a recruit.
            Not a salesperson. Not a friend.
            A machine that expects compliance.

Rules:
- SHORT sentences. Most lines under 10 words.
- ALL CAPS for system-level text (labels, HUD, task IDs).
- Sentence case for human-facing paragraphs.
- NEVER say "we are excited to announce"
- NEVER say "join us on this amazing journey"
- Use double dashes — not em dashes
- Numbers always numeric: 175,000 not "one hundred seventy five thousand"
- Use "::" as namespace separator in labels
